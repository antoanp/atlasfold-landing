Most owners treat reviews as reputation: more stars, better. That is true, but it is
only half the picture.

To Google, every review is a piece of text attached to your business. When a
customer writes "fast tyre change in Lyulin", you have just earned a relevance
signal for exactly that search — for free.

## Ask at the right moment

People leave a review when the emotion is fresh. The ask has to land hours, not
weeks, after the service.

- Send a short message with a direct link to the review form.
- Don't ask for "five stars" — ask for an honest opinion that mentions the service.
- Make it part of the process, not the exception.

## Reply to every review

The reply is your one place to add keywords in natural language.

- Thank them and restate the service and the neighbourhood: "Glad the tyre change in
  Lyulin was quick."
- Reply to the negative ones too — calm and specific. Google and future customers
  are reading.
- Don't paste the same reply every time. Variation reads as more genuine.

## Turn reviews into content

Your best reviews should not live only in the profile.

- Quote them on service and neighbourhood pages.
- Use customers' own phrasing as headings and FAQ questions.
- Track which services get mentioned most — that is free market feedback.
