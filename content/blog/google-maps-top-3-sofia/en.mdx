When someone in Sofia searches for "plumber" or "dentist", Google shows a map with
three results. Those three listings take most of the calls. Everything below them
effectively does not exist.

The good news: ranking in that pack is not a lottery. Google orders local results by
three clear factors. Here they are, and what to do about each.

## Relevance: tell Google exactly what you do

Relevance is how well your profile matches the search. Google reads it from the
categories, description and services in your Google Business Profile — not from what
you happen to know about your business.

- Pick a **primary category** that matches the exact service you want to rank for.
  One primary category, not five generic ones.
- Add every relevant **secondary category**.
- Describe your services in the words customers type into Google — "leak repair",
  not "plumbing works".

## Distance: the proximity you don't fully control

Google shows businesses near the searcher. You cannot move your office, but you can
widen the area you appear in:

- Set an accurate **service area** in your profile.
- Build separate pages for the neighbourhoods you serve — Lozenets, Mladost,
  Lyulin. Each page speaks to that specific district.
- Mention local landmarks and streets in your content.

## Prominence: the trust signals

Prominence is your business's reputation in Google's eyes. It builds slowly and is
the hardest to fake — which is why it carries a lot of weight.

- **Reviews.** A steady stream of new reviews is a stronger signal than 200 old
  ones. Ask every happy customer.
- **Reply to every review** — using the words you want to rank for.
- **Citations.** The same name, address and phone number across directories all over
  the Bulgarian web.

## What to expect, and when

Profile changes take effect within weeks. Reviews and citations compound over
months. A realistic horizon for a durable Top 3 spot for a competitive service in
Sofia is about 90 days of consistent work.
