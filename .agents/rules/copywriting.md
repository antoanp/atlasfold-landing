# Copywriting & Translation Rules

## Primary Language: Bulgarian (bg)
All copy must be written first in Bulgarian. This is the primary audience.
Natural, colloquial Bulgarian — not formal or corporate. Direct address (ти/вие — use "Вие" for respect).

## Secondary Language: English (en)
English copy mirrors Bulgarian in meaning but is adapted for English-speaking readers.
Not a literal translation — a cultural adaptation.

## Tone of Voice
- Direct and confident — not pushy
- Specific — use numbers, timeframes, guarantees ("90 дни", "Топ 3", "75%")
- Empathy first — speak to the pain (invisible on Google = losing customers)
- Zero jargon — business owners don't know what "backlinks" are; say "authority signals"
- Short sentences. Strong verbs. No passive voice.

## Key Messages (use these consistently)
- Core promise: "Топ 3 в Google за 90 дни — гарантирано"
- Fear hook: "75% от търсенията отиват при първите 3 резултата. Ако не сте там — не съществувате."
- Differentiator: "Без рекламен бюджет. Без плащане на клик. Дългосрочен резултат."
- Risk reversal: "Ако не постигнем резултат — не плащате."

## Translation Key Conventions
Use flat, descriptive dot-notation:
- nav.links.beforeAfter
- hero.headline
- hero.subheadline
- hero.stats.clients
- advantage.title
- pricing.standard.guarantee
- faq.question1.title

## NEVER
- Hardcode Bulgarian or English strings in .tsx files
- Use lorem ipsum
- Write generic marketing fluff without specific claims
- Mix languages within a single JSON file
